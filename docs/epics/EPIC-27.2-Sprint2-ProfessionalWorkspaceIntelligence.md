# Version 27.2 — Sprint 2: Professional Workspace Intelligence

## Objective

Make the professional workspace more informative and interactive before broker integration.

## Delivered Capabilities

- Workspace profile selector.
- Multi-watchlist dashboard.
- Opportunity heat map.
- Score gauges.
- Opportunity history panel.
- Mini sparklines for score movement.
- Keyboard navigation:
  - Alt+Up selects previous ranked result.
  - Alt+Down selects next ranked result.
- LocalStorage persistence for workspace intelligence state.

## Manual Validation

1. Open `/scanner`.
2. Confirm Workspace Profile panel appears.
3. Confirm Watchlist Dashboard appears.
4. Run scan.
5. Confirm Opportunity Heat Map appears.
6. Confirm Opportunity History appears.
7. Click heat map cells and history rows.
8. Use Alt+Up / Alt+Down to move through ranked results.
9. Confirm no console errors.
