# Version 26.3 — Sprint 3: Live Auto-Refresh & Background Scanning

## Objective

Turn the scanner from a manual scan tool into a continuously running market monitor.

## Delivered Capabilities

- Start and pause live scanning.
- Configurable refresh intervals.
- Countdown to next scan.
- Last scan timestamp.
- Scan history diagnostics.
- Rank movement tracking.
- New opportunity highlighting.
- CSV export includes rank movement.

## Manual Validation

1. Open `/scanner`.
2. Click **Run Scan** once.
3. Click **Start Live**.
4. Confirm countdown updates.
5. Confirm repeated scans run automatically.
6. Confirm diagnostics update.
7. Click **Pause**.
8. Confirm live scanning stops.
