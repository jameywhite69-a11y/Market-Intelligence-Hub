# Version 27.0 — Sprint 2.1: Decision Panel UX Compression

## Objective

Make the Institutional Decision panel compact, readable, and practical as a trading workstation side panel.

## Delivered Capabilities

- Sticky decision summary.
- Compact decision score.
- Compact recommendation chips.
- Collapsible decision summary, reasons, concerns, and factors.
- Collapsible Confidence Engine section.
- Collapsible Trade Planning Engine section.
- Collapsible Strategy Score section.
- Collapsible Technical Engine section.
- Internal right-panel scrolling.
- Reduced vertical repetition.

## Manual Validation

1. Open `/scanner`.
2. Run a scan.
3. Click a result.
4. Confirm the right panel shows a compact Institutional Decision card.
5. Expand and collapse:
   - Decision Summary
   - Top Reasons
   - Concerns
   - Primary Factors
   - Confidence Engine
   - Trade Planning Engine
   - Strategy Score
   - Technical Engine
6. Confirm no browser console errors.
